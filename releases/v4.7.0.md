# BagOfGold 4.7.0

BagOfGold 4.7.0 moves physical-currency accounting behind the public
CustomItemsLib service contract and strengthens balance persistence.

## Highlights

- Registers balance accounting and Shopkeepers slot policy through Bukkit's `ServicesManager`.
- Preserves physical money during reconnects and orders pending writes before new reads.
- Counts and reconciles money inside nested portable containers.
- Keeps Shopkeepers prepared and manually inserted payments stable during trades.
- Retains CMILib as the maintained action-bar and boss-bar backend.
- Preserves existing currency metadata, debt behavior and storage formats.

## Compatibility

- Java 21.
- Paper 1.21.11 is the validated runtime.
- CustomItemsLib 1.3.0 is required.
- MobHunting 8.7.0 is the validated companion release.

## Validation

- All automated balance and storage tests passed.
- Reconnects, game-mode changes, loose and nested money, Shopkeepers purchases and change were tested in game.
- Complete-stack startup, shutdown and database persistence completed without BagOfGold errors.
